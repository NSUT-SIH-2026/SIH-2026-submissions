# SIH 2026 Project Submission Portal

Welcome to the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 Project Submission Portal!

## 📝 How to Submit Your Project

Follow these simple steps to submit your SIH 2026 project:

### Step 1: Open the Submission Link
Click the link below to start your submission:
👉 **[Submit Your Project Here](https://github.com/NSUT-SIH-2026/SIH-2026-submissions/issues/new/choose)**

### Step 2: Select the Submission Form
You will see the form option:
- **🚀 SIH Project Submission** ← Click this option

### Step 3: Fill All Required Fields
Complete all the fields marked with an asterisk (*):
- Team Name
- Team Leader Details
- Contact Information
- Team Members
- Problem Statement Details
- Project Description
- Technology Stack
- Repository Link
- Presentation File/Link
- Demo Video Link
- College/Institute Name
- Accept the Declaration

### Step 4: Upload or Link Your Presentation
For your PPT/Presentation:
- You can drag and drop files directly into the form
- If your file is too large, upload it to **Google Drive** or **OneDrive** and paste the shareable link

### Step 5: Submit
Click the **"Submit new issue"** button to complete your submission.

---

## ✅ What Happens After Submission?

Once you submit:
1. Your submission will be created as a GitHub Issue
2. Our team will review your project details
3. You will receive updates via GitHub notifications
4. Keep an eye on this repository for announcements

---

## ❓ Important Notes

- **All fields are required** - Please fill in all information accurately
- **Declaration is mandatory** - Confirm that your project follows SIH guidelines
- **Shareable Links** - Ensure any Google Drive or OneDrive links are accessible
- **No Need for Technical Knowledge** - You don't need to know GitHub or coding to submit
- **One Submission per Team** - Use your team leader's details for the submission

---

## 🎯 SIH 2026 Guidelines

Ensure your project:
- Addresses the selected Problem Statement
- Demonstrates innovation and practicality
- Is original work by your team
- Complies with all SIH rules and regulations

## 📞 Need Help?

For any issues with submission, contact your institution's SIH coordinator.
